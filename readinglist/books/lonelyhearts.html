The Day of the Locust
Notes & quotes I try to include anything I underline

Similar to Miss Lonelyhearts, everything ends in dispair for Tod Hackett. The ending pages of the book seem prophetic for America today, as the American dream, as it seemed then, is dead. I enjoyed this. You can tell West has developped his narrative skills after writing Miss Lonelyhearts. Many of the images in this book will remain resonant; Harry's slow death, the rooster fight, and the ensuing final scenes with Homer Simpson and Tod. It was also really awesome to haphazardly come across the (partial) inspiration for the namesake of Matt Groening's Homer Simpson. All in all, a vivid read about the decline of American society in mid-century Hollywood.
