The insignia of the PLAC, and the former People's Library, is the Angel Hnih. In Khouzan mythology, Angels are sacred protectors. In this case, Hnih is the guardian of knowledge.

Welcome to the official site of the Public Library and Archive of Calvinvill
Formerly known as the People's Library of Calvinvill.

This site was an act of Robert Chubbles and an incentive of the Public Library of Calvinvill (PLAC) under the Cultural and Historical Placemaking Act. The PLAC and its resources are always free and open to the public.

The purpose of this site is to serve as an online resource for any information on the Kouz Islands and the larger world. For information on the creation of this site, see Metahistory.
